counting at 260 ft upstream). No inductive loops at either site. Source: `intersections/<id>/config/detectors.csv`.

## Not published by ATSPM (would need GDOT or the radar's own setup file)

Where each zone is and how long it is; the radar's settings (output delay / extend, presence hold for stopped
vehicles); how the controller uses each input (call, extend, delay, locking or not); exact lane markings. The
most useful single item would be the Wavetronix Matrix zone configuration for each site.

## Right turns

Right turn on red is allowed almost everywhere, so right turns will have to be inferred at every site. 137 has
right-turn zones only on the cross street (presence-type); EB/WB rights are inside the through-right counts.
7130 has no right-turn detection; rights can be bounded from the exit counts (each exit = one through + one left +
one right from three different approaches).
