# Results: SIG 7065 controller comparison (validation weekdays)

Ready-to-plot tables and the slide figures. Everything here is regenerated by

    uv run python atsc/sites/7065/analyze.py

from the raw runs in `../eval/` (`hour_mv.csv`, `mech.csv`, `conditions.csv`). Do not edit these files by hand:
change the analysis and rerun it. Each CSV is tidy (one row per observation), and units are in the column names.

## What was run

- **Days and hours.** 20 validation weekdays, full days. Only clean hours are used: every 5-min count bin of the
  hour was measured, with no detector outage. An hour is kept only if every compared controller has it.
- **Rule controllers** (field plan, Webster, actuated, MaxPressure): 3 simulation seeds each.
- **Learnt controllers:** 5 training seeds × 3 simulation seeds each. A learner's value for an hour is the
  mean over all its runs.
- **Traffic condition** of an hour comes from GDOT's own counts. Y is the sum of the critical flow ratios
  (flow / (lanes × 1,950 veh/h)) on the ring-barrier critical path:

  | Condition | Y |
  |---|---|
  | light | < 0.15 |
  | moderate | 0.15–0.30 |
  | busy | 0.30–0.40 |
  | heavy | ≥ 0.40 |

- **Delay** = SUMO time loss + time waiting to enter the network, per vehicle, scored on the vehicles GDOT
  counted in that hour.

## Columns

**`delay_by_hour.csv`**

- `mean_delay_s_per_veh`: mean delay of all counted vehicles in the hour (s/veh).
- `worst_movement_p95_delay_s`: the 95th-percentile delay of the worst-off movement in that hour (s). This is a
  fairness measure.
- `condition`, `Y`: the hour's traffic condition (see above).

**`summary_by_condition.csv`**

- `*_ci_low`, `*_ci_high`: 95 % interval from a bootstrap over days. Hours of the same day are not
  independent, so days are resampled.
- The values are means of daily means, so each day counts equally and each hour within a day counts equally.
  The overall vehicle-weighted delay is in `runs_overall.csv`.

**`paired_vs_maxpressure.csv`**

- `diff_vs_maxpressure_s_per_veh`: the controller's delay minus MaxPressure's in the same hour, averaged per
  day and then over days. Negative means less delay than MaxPressure.
- `wilcoxon_p`: Wilcoxon signed-rank test over the 20 daily differences, two-sided.
- `significant`: p < 0.05 **and** the interval excludes 0. There is no correction for multiple tests: about 50
  are made, so expect about 2 false positives.

**`runs_overall.csv`**

- `overall_delay_s_per_veh`: delay over all clean hours of the 20 days, weighted by vehicles.
- `training_seed` is empty for rule controllers. `lstdq` (v1, training seed 0) also has 3 simulation seeds.

**`mechanism_by_condition.csv`**

- `mean_cycle_s`: mean cycle length.
- `mean_green_s`: mean green of the phase.
- `greens_per_cycle`: 1.0 means the phase is served every cycle; below 1 means it is skipped (no demand, or the
  controller skips it).
- `share_ended_at_max_green`: share of greens that ran to max green.

**`lstdq_v2f_learnt_rule.csv`**

- The controller ends a green when (w_end − w_extend) · state > 0.
- `effect_toward_ending_mean` is that weight multiplied by the feature's typical variation (its standard
  deviation in the controller's own decisions).
  - Positive: a typical increase of the feature pushes towards ending the green.
  - Negative: it pushes towards extending.
  - `bias` is the raw weight.
- Features, per phase (`P1`–`P8`):
  - `occ`: stop-bar occupancy.
  - `gap`: time since the last vehicle (as gap-out uses).
  - `rate`: arrivals in the last 30 s.
  - `queue`: queue estimate.
- Plus: the other ring's current phase (one-hot), its elapsed green, and the serving phase's own elapsed green.
- `seeds_agreeing_in_sign`: how many of the 5 training seeds agree. 5/5 means the effect is reliable; 3/5 is
  noise.

## Limitations (state them with any figure)

- **Coordination.** 7065 runs in GDOT's coordinated Peachtree group (common cycle with 7066, 7071, 7058). The
  model is isolated, with random arrivals and no platoons, so the field plan pays coordination's cost without
  its benefit.
- **Validation.** The simulation is not yet accepted by its own validation (`../VALIDATION.md`): average split
  (V1), % gap-outs (V2) and overflow days (V5) fail, mostly on the left-turn phases.
- **Counts.**
  - The P2 count (Peachtree from the NE) is suspect: about half of what the neighbouring signals imply.
  - Right turns are not counted by GDOT, so they carry no simulated demand.
- **NFQ.** The neural learner (NFQ) is untuned and did not converge (`../METHODS.md` amendment 1). It is a
  weak comparator, not evidence that neural control cannot work here.
